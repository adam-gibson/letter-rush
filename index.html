Letter Rush

The quick-thinking party word game. One letter, six categories – can you fill them all?

You will need paper and a pen for each player.

How to play

  1. Choose six categories, or press Randomise. Set the winning score.
  2. Draw a letter. Agree a time limit, such as two minutes.
  3. Write one answer per category, each beginning with that letter. For example, for B: Banana, Blazer, Brazil…
  4. Read your answers out. Score 1 point for each answer that nobody else wrote. Duplicates and blanks score nothing.
  5. Draw the next letter and play again. The first player to reach the target score wins.

Tips

  • Redraw swaps a letter nobody likes. It will not come up again this game.
  • Letters never repeat in a game. Q, X and Z are left out unless you choose otherwise.
  • Presentation mode shows the letter and categories full-screen for a projector or TV, and keeps the screen awake.
  • Add your own categories under My categories. Sign in to use them on all your devices.
  • Install the app to play offline.
